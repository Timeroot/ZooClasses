content: "BPP⊆(NP,P-samplable)"
ref: "BPP ⊆ (NP,P-samplable): any BPP language is in NP (BPP ⊆ NP under some conditions), and BPP problems with the uniform distribution form an instance of (NP,P-samplable). More precisely, BPP ⊆ (NP,P-samplable) as the class of distributional problems where the language is in NP and the distribution is P-samplable."
