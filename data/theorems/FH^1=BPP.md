The forward direction (that FH^1⊆BPP) is given by the perspective on {lang:BPP} as a reversible polynomial-size circuit with input bits, zeroed bits, and uniformly random input bits. The reverse direction is given by simulating the one layer of Hadamard gates probabilistically - which is safe because no interference or cancellation can later occur."
