Weighted Analogue of NP. The class of decision problems of the form (x, k) (k a parameter), that are fixed-parameter reducible to:

**Weighted 3SAT**: Given a 3SAT formula, does it have a satisfying assignment of Hamming weight k?

A fixed-parameter reduction is a Turing reduction running in time f(k)·p(|x|) for arbitrary f and polynomial p, where all queried instances have parameter ≤ k.

Contains FPT. Defined in {ref:df99}. If FPT = W[1], then NP ⊆ DTIME(2^{o(n)}).

Generalizes to {lang:W[t]}.
