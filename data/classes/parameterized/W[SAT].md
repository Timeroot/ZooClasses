Weighted Satisfiability. The class of decision problems of the form (x, k) (k a parameter), fixed-parameter reducible to:

**Weighted SAT**: Given a Boolean formula F (with no restriction on depth), does F have a satisfying assignment of Hamming weight k?

See {lang:W[1]} for the definition of fixed-parameter reducibility. Defined in {ref:df99}.

Contains W[t] for every t, and is contained in {lang:W[P]}.
