W[t] With Parameter-Dependent Depth. Same as {lang:W[t]}, except that the circuit depth can depend on the parameter k rather than being a fixed constant. The number of unbounded-fanin gates along any path to the root is still at most t.

W^*[1] = W[1] {ref:dft96}. W^*[2] = W[2] {ref:df97}. The problem is open for larger t.
