Determinant. The class of decision problems reducible in L to the problem of computing the determinant of an n×n matrix of n-bit integers.

Defined in {ref:coo85}. Contained in {lang:NC^2}, and contains NL and {lang:PL} {ref:bcp83}. Graph Isomorphism is hard for DET under L-reductions {ref:tor00}.

The corresponding function class equals {lang:GapL}: the determinant of integer matrices is GapL-complete.
