Semi-Efficient Parallel. The class of problems in P for which the best parallel algorithm (using a polynomial number of processors) is faster than the best serial algorithm by a factor of Ω(n^ε) for some ε > 0.

Defined in {ref:krs90}.

SP is also an alternate name for {lang:XP_uniform}.
