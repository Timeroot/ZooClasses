Communication Complexity ⊕P. The communication complexity analog of ⊕P.

Not contained in UPP^cc {ref:for02}. Does not contain ZPP^cc if partial functions are allowed {ref:gpw16b}.

The complexity measure associated with ⊕P^cc is equivalent to the log of the rank of the communication matrix over GF(2).
