Complement of Randomized Logspace. The class of problems whose "no" instances have a randomized logspace algorithm (with one-sided error, no errors on "yes" instances).

Equals RL under the standard (one-sided error) definition. (Any randomized logspace algorithm for a language L can be adapted to recognize the complement.) See {lang:RL}.
