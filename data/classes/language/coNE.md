name: coNE
properties:
  - complement
---
Complement of {lang:NE}. A language L is in coNE if and only if its complement L̄ is in NE, i.e., NE = NTIME(2^O(n)). Equivalently, coNE is the class of languages whose "no" instances can be verified in nondeterministic exponential time with linear exponent.

coNE is contained in NEEXP and contains coE. By padding arguments, the question of whether NE = coNE is related to (but not equivalent to) NP = coNP.
