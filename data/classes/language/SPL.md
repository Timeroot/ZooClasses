Stoic PL. Has the same relation to {lang:PL} as SPP does to PP: an NL machine where the number of accepting paths differs by 1 (yes vs. no cases).

Contains {lang:UL}. Contained in {lang:C_eqL} and Mod_kL.

Contains the maximum matching and perfect matching problems under a pseudorandom assumption {ref:arz99}.

Equals the set of problems low for {lang:GapL}.
