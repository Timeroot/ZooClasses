Verification Class with OR. The class of languages that have verification presentable as the OR of m instances of SAT, each of size n (where m is the witness length and n is the instance length).

VC_0 ⊆ VC_or ⊆ VC_1. See {lang:VC_k} for context. Introduced in {ref:hn06}.
