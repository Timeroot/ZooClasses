One-Sided Analog of {lang:AWPP}. Same as {lang:SBP}, except that f is a nonnegative-valued {lang:GapP} function rather than a #P function.

Defined in {ref:vya03}, where the following was also shown: A_0PP contains {lang:QMA}, {lang:AWPP}, and {lang:coC_=P}; and A_0PP is contained in {lang:PP}.

Kuperberg {ref:kup09} showed that A_0PP = {lang:SBQP}.
