Semi-Unbounded-Fanin AC^0. SAC^k is the class of decision problems solvable by a family of depth-O(log^k n) circuits with unbounded-fanin OR gates and bounded-fanin (fan-in 2) AND gates. Negations are allowed only at the input level. A uniformity condition may also be imposed. SAC^k sits "halfway" between NC^k and AC^k.

SAC^0 strictly contains NC^0 and is strictly contained in AC^0. Under standard uniformity, SAC^0 = NC^1.
