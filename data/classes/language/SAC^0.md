SAC^k is the class of decision problems solvable by a family of depth-O(logkn) circuits with unbounded-fanin OR & bounded-fanin AND gates. Negations are only allowed at the input level. A uniformity condition may also be imposed. SAC^k sits naturally 'halfway' between NC^k and AC^k.
