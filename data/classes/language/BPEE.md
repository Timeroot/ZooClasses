Bounded-Error Probabilistic Double-Exponential Time with Linear Exponent. The class of decision problems solvable by a probabilistic Turing machine in time 2^(2^O(n)) with error probability at most 1/3 on all inputs.

Contains EE = DTIME(2^(2^O(n))), and is contained in EEXP.
