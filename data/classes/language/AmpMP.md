Amplifiable MP. The class of decision problems such that for some #P function f(x, 0^m), (1) the answer on input x is 'yes' if and only if the middle bit of f(x) is 1, and (2) the m bits of f(x) to the left and right of the middle bit are all 0.

Defined in {ref:gkr95}. Contains {lang:PH} and {lang:ModPH}. Contained in {lang:MP}.
