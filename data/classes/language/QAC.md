Quantum AC. The class of decision problems solvable by polylogarithmic-depth, polynomial-size uniform quantum circuit. Here each layer of the circuit is a tensor product of one-qubit gates and Toffoli gates, or is a tensor product of controlled-NOT gates. Quantum generalization of AC^0. Quantum generalization of AC.
