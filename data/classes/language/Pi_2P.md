Universal polynomial time, 2 alternations. coNP^NP, the second universal level of the polynomial hierarchy PH. A language L is in Π_2P if there is a polynomial-time predicate R such that x ∈ L iff ∀y ∃z R(x,y,z). Complement of Σ_2P.

BPP ⊆ Σ_2P ∩ Π_2P {ref:lau83}. AM ⊆ Π_2P.
