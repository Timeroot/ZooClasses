Deterministic Context-Free Languages. The class of languages accepted by deterministic pushdown automata.

Defined in {ref:gg66}, where it was also shown that DCFL is strictly contained in {lang:CFL} {ref:bra77}, is contained in {lang:UCFL} (also strictly), and strictly contains {lang:REG}.
