P With O(log n) NP Queries. Also known as Θ_2P. The class of decision problems solvable by a P machine making O(log n) queries to an NP oracle.

Equals P^{||NP} (P with polynomially many nonadaptive NP queries) {ref:bh91} {ref:hem89}. Contained in {lang:PP} {ref:bhw89}. Contains P^{NP[k]} for all constants k.

Determining the winner of Dodgson's 1876 election system is P^{NP[log]}-complete {ref:hhr97}.
