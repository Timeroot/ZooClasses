Difference Polynomial-Time. DP = BH_2, the second level of the Boolean hierarchy. The class of languages that are the intersection of an NP language and a coNP language.

Contains NP and coNP, is contained in Δ_2P. Complete problems include Exact-Clique and SAT-UNSAT. Defined in {ref:py84}.
