Approximable in Polynomial Time. (Usually called "AP" in the literature; renamed here to avoid confusion with {lang:AP}.)

The class of real-valued functions from {0,1}^n to [0,1] that can be approximated within any ε > 0 by a deterministic Turing machine in time polynomial in both n and 1/ε.

Defined in {ref:krc00}, where it was also shown that the set of AxP machines is in {lang:RE}.
