Polynomially-Bounded L_∞^{-1} Spectral Norm. The class of Boolean functions f:{-1,1}^n → {-1,1} such that the maximum of |α|^{-1}, over all Fourier coefficients α of f, is upper-bounded by a polynomial in n.

Defined in {ref:bs90}, where it was shown that PL_∞ strictly contains {lang:PT_1}.
