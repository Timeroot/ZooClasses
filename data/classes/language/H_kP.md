High Hierarchy in NP. The class of problems A in NP such that Σ_kP^A = Σ_{k+1}P; that is, adding A as an oracle increases the power of the k-th level of the polynomial hierarchy by a maximum amount.

For all k, H_k is contained in H_{k+1}.

H_0 consists exactly of the problems complete for NP under Cook reductions.

H_1 consists exactly of the problems complete for NP under strong non-deterministic Turing reductions {ref:sch83}.

Defined in {ref:sch83}. See also {lang:L_kP}.
