NPMV_t-Selective Sets. The class of total nondeterministic multivalued functions f ∈ NPMV_t (defined on every input) for which there exists a polynomial-time computable selector: given any two inputs x, y, the selector outputs either x or y and the selected element's image under f is nonempty.

The total-function analogue of {lang:NPMV-sel}: the same selector condition but restricted to total NPMV functions (where a value always exists).
