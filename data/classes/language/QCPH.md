Quantum Classical Polynomial Hierarchy. A bounded-error quantum generalization of {lang:PH} in which all proofs are classical and the verifier is a quantum circuit.

Defined in {ref:gsssy18}. Contained in P^{PP^{PP}} {ref:gsssy18}. Contains {lang:PH} and is contained in {lang:QPH} {ref:gy24}.
