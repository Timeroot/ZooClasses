Continuous Logarithmic-Time. Roughly, the class of continuous problems solvable by an ordinary differential equation (ODE) with convergence time logarithmic in the input size. The vector field is specified by an NC^1 formula, with n parameters representing the input. The point to which the ODE converges is the output.

Defined in {ref:bsf02}. Finding the maximum of n integers is in CLOG, so CLOG is best thought of as the continuous-time analog of NC^1. Contained in {lang:CP}.
