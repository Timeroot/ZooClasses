Complement of {lang:RP}. The class of problems solvable by a randomized polynomial-time algorithm with one-sided error: if the answer is "yes", the algorithm always accepts; if the answer is "no", the algorithm rejects with probability at least 1/2.

Contains primality testing {ref:ss77}. ZPP = RP ∩ coRP.
