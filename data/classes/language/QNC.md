The class of decision problems solvable by polylogarithmic-depth quantum circuits with bounded probability of error. The zoo says, "A uniformity condition may also be imposed."; for the purpose of this site we _do_ impose that condition. Some sources like https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3570637 refer to this as BQNC.
