Tensor Isomorphism. The class of problems polynomial-time Turing reducible to Tensor Isomorphism. Defined in {ref:gq19}.

The relationship between TI over different fields is an open question, but many reductions hold over any field.

Over any field F, TI contains GI. Tensor Isomorphism itself (over finite fields) is in NP and coAM (and in fact SZK; the same holds over arbitrary fields in the BSS model). So if Tensor Isomorphism is NP-complete, PH collapses.

Many natural problems are TI-complete, such as isomorphism of algebras and modules.
