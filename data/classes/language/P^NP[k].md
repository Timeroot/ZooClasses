P With k NP Queries (for constant k). Equals P with 2^k - 1 parallel queries to NP (i.e., queries that do not depend on prior answers) {ref:bh91} {ref:hem89}.

If P^{NP[1]} = P^{NP[2]}, then P^{NP[1]} = P^{NP[log]} and PH collapses to Δ_3P (Kadin; attributed in {ref:har87b}).
