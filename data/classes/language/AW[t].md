Alternating W[t]. Has the same relation to {lang:W[t]} as PSPACE does to NP.

Same as {lang:AW[SAT]}, except that the formula F has depth at most t. Defined in {ref:df99}.

Contained in {lang:AW[*]}. DFT98 {ref:dft98} showed that for all t, AW[t] = AW[*].
