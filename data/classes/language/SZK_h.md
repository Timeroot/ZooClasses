SZK With Limited Help. The class of decision problems for which a "yes" answer can be verified by a statistical zero-knowledge proof protocol, and the prover and verifier both have access to a string computed by a trusted probabilistic polynomial-time third party with access to the input.

Defined in {ref:bg03}, where it was also shown that SZK_h = {lang:SZK}.
