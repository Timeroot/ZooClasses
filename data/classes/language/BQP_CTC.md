BQP with Closed Time Curves. Same as BQP with access to two sets of qubits: causality-respecting qubits and CTC (closed-time-curve) qubits.

Defined in {ref:aar05c}, where it was shown PSPACE ⊆ BQP_CTC ⊆ SQG = PSPACE. Therefore BQP_CTC = PSPACE {ref:aw09}. See also {lang:P_CTC}.
