Semi-Unbounded-Fanin AC. SAC^k is the class of decision problems solvable by a family of depth-O(log^k n) circuits with unbounded-fanin OR gates and bounded-fanin (fan-in 2) AND gates. Negations are only allowed at the input level. A uniformity condition may also be imposed.

Defined in {ref:bcd89}, where it was shown SAC^k is closed under complement for every k > 0.

See {lang:SAC^0} and {lang:SAC^1} for specific levels.
