Randomized Nick's Class. The class of decision problems solvable by polylogarithmic-depth polynomial-size random circuits with bounded one-sided error: it must never incorrectly accept (no false positives), and it must accept correctly with probability at least 1/2 (for "yes" inputs).

Analogous to how RP relates to P. Contains perfect matching (Schwartz-Zippel; Mulmuley-Vazirani-Vazirani algorithm).
