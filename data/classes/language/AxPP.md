Approximable in Probabilistic Polynomial Time. (Usually called "APP" in the literature; renamed here to avoid confusion with {lang:APP}.)

The class of real-valued functions from {0,1}^n to [0,1] that can be approximated within any ε > 0 by a probabilistic Turing machine in time polynomial in both n and 1/ε.

Defined in {ref:krc00}. Approximating the acceptance probability of a Boolean circuit is AxPP-complete. If AxPP = {lang:AxP}, then {lang:BPP} = {lang:P}. AxPP is recursively enumerable {ref:jer07}.
