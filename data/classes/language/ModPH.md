Modular Counting Hierarchy. The closure of P under the ∃, ∀, and Mod_k operators. Equivalently, the smallest class C such that NP^C, coNP^C, and Mod_kP^C are all contained in C for each k. Introduced in {ref:gkr95}.

Contains {lang:PH} and Mod_kP for each k. Contained in, and low for, {lang:AmpMP} and {lang:MP}.

For fixed m, the restriction to only Mod_k for k = m is denoted ModPH_m {ref:buss17}. By Toda's theorem, for prime m, ModPH_m = BP·Mod_mP.
