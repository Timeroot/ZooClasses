Unbounded Fanin Linear-Size Constant-Depth Circuits. The class of decision problems solvable by a nonuniform family of Boolean circuits with a linear number of wires, constant depth, and unbounded fanin.

Contained in LC^0, and therefore strictly contained in AC^0.
