Randomized Halting Logspace. The class of problems decidable by a logspace randomized Turing machine that halts on every input for every setting of the random tape (a strict "always-halting" requirement). If the answer is "yes", at least 2/3 of random tape choices lead to acceptance; if the answer is "no", all random tape choices lead to rejection.

Contained in RL, where the weaker condition—halting with probability 1—suffices. The strict halting requirement makes R_HL a proper subclass of RL.
