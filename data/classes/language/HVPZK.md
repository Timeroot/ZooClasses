Honest-Verifier PZK. The class of decision problems that have {lang:PZK} protocols assuming an honest verifier (one who does not try to learn more about the problem by deviating from the protocol).

Contained in {lang:PP} {ref:bhctv17}. There is an oracle where HVPZK is not closed under complement {ref:bhctv17}.
