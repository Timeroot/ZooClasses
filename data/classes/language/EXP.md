Exponential Time. Equals DTIME(2^p(n)) over all polynomials p. Also equals P with an E oracle.

If L = P then PSPACE = EXP. If EXP ⊆ P/poly then EXP = MA {ref:bfl91}.

There exist oracles relative to which EXP = NP = ZPP {ref:hel84}, EXP = NEXP but P ≠ NP {ref:dek76}, and EXP ≠ PSPACE {ref:dek76}.
