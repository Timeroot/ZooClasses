Level 2 of NC. The class of decision problems solvable by uniform Boolean circuits with depth O(log^2 n) and fan-in 2. See {lang:NC} for context.

Contains {lang:AC^1} and {lang:DET}, both of which contain NL. Currently no problem is known to be in NC^2 but not in AC^1 ∪ DET.
