---
name: coSL
properties:
  - complement
---
Complement of {lang:SL}. A language L is in coSL if and only if its complement L̄ is in SL.

Since SL = coSL {ref:nt95} (the symmetric logspace hierarchy collapses), and furthermore SL = L {ref:rei04} (Reingold's theorem), coSL = L as well. This entry is kept for historical completeness.
