Strict Quantum RP. The class of problems in NP whose witnesses are in FBQP. For example, the set of square-free numbers is in coRBQP using only the fact that factoring is in FBQP (the factorization proves there is a square divisor, even without proving the factors are prime).

Contains {lang:RP} and {lang:ZBQP}. Contained in {lang:BQP} and {lang:RQP}.

Defined here to clarify {lang:EQP}; see also {lang:ZBQP}.
