Bounded-Error Probabilistic L with Two-Way Access to Randomness. Languages decided with two-sided bounded error by a logspace machine with a read-only tape containing random bits. In contrast with {lang:BPL}, the random bits can be read multiple times without storing them in working space.

It is unknown if BP•L is contained in P {ref:nis93}. Contained in {lang:BPP}. Contains {lang:BPL} and ZP•L.
