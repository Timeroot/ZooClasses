One-Way NAuxPDA^p. A one-way variant of {lang:NAuxPDA^p}: nondeterministic log-space polynomial-time Turing machines with an auxiliary pushdown, but where the input tape is read one-way.

Defined in {ref:bra77}, where it was also shown that 1NAuxPDA^p strictly contains CFL and is strictly contained in LOGCFL. The class corresponds to the closure of CFL under one-way logspace reductions.
