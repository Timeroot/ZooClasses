Quantum NC^1. The class of decision problems solvable by logarithmic-depth bounded-error quantum circuits. (A uniformity condition may also be imposed.)

See {lang:QNC} for context. In contrast to {lang:NC^1}, it is not clear how to simulate QNC^1 on a quantum computer with one pure qubit and the rest maximally mixed {ref:asv00}.
