Quantum Interactive Proofs with 2 rounds, a restricted form {lang:QIP}. Compare with the note on {lang:AM} about restricted rounds. It turns out that QIP[k>=3]=QIP[3]=QIP, and QIP[1] is by definition QMA, so the only other interesting case is QIP[2].
