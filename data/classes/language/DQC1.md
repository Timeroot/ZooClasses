Deterministic Quantum Computing with 1 Clean Bit. The class of problems solvable by a BQP machine in which a single qubit is initialized to the '0' state, and the remaining qubits are initialized to the maximally mixed state. Defined in {ref:KL98}. This has been called BQ1P, sometimes, to distinguish it from the _sampling_ problem of DQC1 circuits. Since "DQC1" seems to have stuck, we call the sampling problem DQC1S.
