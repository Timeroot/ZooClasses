Almost WPP. The class of decision problems solvable by an NP machine such that for some FP function f, (1) if the answer is "no", then the difference between accepting and rejecting paths is nonneg and at most 2^{-poly(n)} f(x), and (2) if the answer is "yes", then the difference is between (1 - 2^{-poly(n)}) f(x) and f(x).

Defined in {ref:ffk94}. Contains {lang:BQP} {ref:fr98}, {lang:WAPP} {ref:bgm02}, {lang:LWPP}, and {lang:WPP}. Contained in {lang:APP} {ref:fen02}.
