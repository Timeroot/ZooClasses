Subexponentially-Solvable Search Problems. The class of FNP search problems solvable in O(2^{εn}) time for every ε > 0.

Defined in {ref:ipz01}, where reductions were given showing that if any of k-SAT, k-colorability, k-set cover, clique, or vertex cover is in SE, then all of them are.
