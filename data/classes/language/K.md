Feasibly Recursive Functions. A class of number-theoretic functions, defined as the closure of basic integer arithmetic operations (+, -, ·, ⌊x/y⌋, as well as constants 0, 1, and projections) under composition and polynomially long sums and products.

Defined in {ref:con73} (who mistakenly claimed K coincides with FP). Equals U_D-uniform FTC^0 {ref:hes01}.
