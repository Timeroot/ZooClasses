Quantum AC^0. The class of decision problems solvable by constant-depth, polynomial-size uniform quantum circuit. Here each layer of the circuit is a tensor product of one-qubit gates and Toffoli gates, or is a tensor product of controlled-NOT gates. Quantum generalization of AC^0. It's unknown whether it contains AC^0.
