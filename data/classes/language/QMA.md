Quantum Merlin-Arthur. Problems for which an omniscient 'Merlin' can submit a proof to 'Arthur' that a statement is true, and Arthur believes it with high probability (<1/3 chance of failing) -- but this proof can be a quantum state, and Arthur has a quantum computer.
