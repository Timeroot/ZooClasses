The class of problems in both NP and coNP.

Contains factoring {ref:pra75}. Contains Graph Isomorphism under a circuit lower bound assumption for NE ∩ coNE {ref:mv99}.

Equals P^{NP∩coNP} {ref:bra79}; in particular, if any problem in NP∩coNP is NP-hard under Turing reduction, then NP = coNP.

Equal to Low(NP) = {L : NP^L = NP} {ref:sch83}.
