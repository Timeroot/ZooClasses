Exponential Time With Linear Exponent. Equals DTIME(2^O(n)).

Does not equal NP or PSPACE relative to any oracle {ref:boo72} {ref:boo74}. Problems hard for BPP under Turing reductions have measure 1 in E {ref:as94}.

Contrast with {lang:EXP} = DTIME(2^poly(n)).
