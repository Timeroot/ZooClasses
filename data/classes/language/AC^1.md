Unbounded Fanin Log-Depth Circuits. AC^1 consists of the problems solvable by a nonuniform family of polynomial-size, O(log n)-depth circuits with unbounded-fanin AND, OR, and NOT gates. See {lang:AC} for the general definition.

Contains {lang:NL} (hence also {lang:NC^1}). Contained in {lang:NC^2}. The class {lang:DET} also satisfies NL ⊆ DET ⊆ NC^2, but no containment is known in either direction between AC^1 and DET.
