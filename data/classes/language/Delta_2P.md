P with NP Oracle. The second level of the polynomial hierarchy PH, defined as P^NP. Contains {lang:BH} and P^{NP[log]}.

A Δ_2P-complete problem: given a Boolean formula, does the lexicographically last satisfying assignment end with a 1? {ref:kre88}.

There exists an oracle relative to which Δ_2P is not contained in PP {ref:bei94}.
