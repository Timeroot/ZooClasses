Existential Theory of the Reals. Problems reducible to the language of multivariate polynomials with integer coefficients that have a real solution. Equivalently, the problem of testing whether a given semialgebraic set is non-empty.

Defined in {ref:sha10}. Contains NP and is contained in PSPACE. Many problems in discrete and computational geometry are complete for this class.
