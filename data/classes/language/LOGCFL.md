The class of decision problems reducible in L to the problem of deciding membership in a context-free language. In order words, the closure under L-reductions of {lang:CFL}. Equivalently, LOGCFL is the class of decision problems solvable by a _uniform_ family of AC1 circuits, in which no AND gate has fan-in exceeding 2, see {ref:Joh90}{ref:Ven91}. When the circuits aren't uniform, we get the LOGCFL/poly = SAC^1, see {lang:SAC^1}.
