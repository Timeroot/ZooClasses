Small Bounded-Error Quantum Polynomial-Time. The class of decision problems for which there exists a polynomial-time quantum algorithm that accepts with probability at least 2^{-p(n)} if the answer is "yes," and with probability at most 2^{-p(n)-1} if the answer is "no," for some polynomial p.

Defined by Kuperberg {ref:kup09}, where it was shown that SBQP = {lang:A_0PP}.
