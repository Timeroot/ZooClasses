Binary Restriction of NP Over the Complex Numbers. The intersection of NP_C (NP over the complex numbers) with {0,1}^* (the set of binary strings).

Contains NP. Contained in PSPACE, and in AM assuming the Extended Riemann Hypothesis {ref:koi96}.
