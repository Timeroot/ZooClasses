Fixed Error Randomized Time. A parameterized class. FERT is the class of decision problems of the form (x, k) decidable in polynomial time by a probabilistic Turing machine such that: (1) if the answer is yes, acceptance probability ≥ 1/2 + min(f(k), 1/|x|^c); (2) if the answer is no, acceptance probability ≤ 1/2.

Defined in {ref:kw15}. Contains BPP and is contained in para-PP and in FPERT.
