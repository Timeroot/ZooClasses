Polynomial Sink. (The S and K stand for "Source" and "Sink.") The class of total function problems of the following form: given a directed graph in which every vertex has in-degree and out-degree at most 1, and given a distinguished source vertex, find a sink (a vertex with out-degree 0).

Defined in {ref:pap90}. Equals {lang:PPADS}.
