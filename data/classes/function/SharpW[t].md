Sharp-W[t]. The analogue of #P for parameterized complexity: the class of parameterized counting problems fixed-parameter parsimonious reducible to #WSAT.

Defined in {ref:fg02}. There exist #W[1]-complete problems whose corresponding decision problems are in FPT {ref:fg02}.

Complete problems for #W[1] include counting paths or cycles of a given length in a graph and counting chordless paths of a given length. Complete problems for #W[2] include the maximal chordless path problem {ref:cf07}.
