Polynomial Parity Argument (Directed). Same as PPA, except the graph is directed, and we are asked to find either a source or a sink.

Defined in {ref:pap94b}. Contained in PPA and PPADS. Nash equilibrium (in a game of two or more players with specified utilities) is in PPAD {ref:pap94b}, and is PPAD-complete with four players {ref:dgp05}, three players {ref:dp05} {ref:cd05}.

There exists an oracle relative to which PPP is not contained in PPAD {ref:bce95} and PPAD is not contained in BQP {ref:li11}.
