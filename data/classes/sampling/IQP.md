Instantaneous Quantum Polynomial-Time. The class of sampling problems solvable by quantum circuits where all gates commute (i.e., diagonal gates in the computational basis). These circuits can be thought of as "instantaneous" since all gates act simultaneously.

Sampling from IQP circuits is conjectured to be hard for classical computers. If a classical computer can efficiently sample from IQP circuits to within multiplicative error, then the polynomial hierarchy collapses.

Related to BosonSampling and other quantum advantage proposals.
