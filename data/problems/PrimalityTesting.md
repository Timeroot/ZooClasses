Given an integer N, determine whether it is prime.

## Variants

### PrimalityLanguage

- **Type:** Language
- **Description:** Given an integer N in binary, determine whether it is prime.

Often written `PRIMES` for the language. In RP (by Miller-Rabin), in co-RP (by Pratt certificates), and in fact in P (by AKS and other algorithms). Hard for TC^0 under AC^0 reductions (by http://ftp.cs.rutgers.edu/pub/allender/primes.pdf)
